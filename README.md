# Room-Escape

Reach the end of room by passing obstacles, avoiding spikes and falling in the water without losing time.

Make sure to open doors that have green light.

Reach the end of room in classic mode, and get high score for infinite mode.

## Game Features:

1. Two modes: Classic and Infinite.
2. Infinite classic levels.
3. Achievements and Daily quests.
4. Statistics
5. You can buy and choose different textures for obstacles and room.
6. Save your data and play across multiple devices without losing data.
7. Leaderboards where you can compete with other players.
